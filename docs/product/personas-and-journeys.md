# Personas and User Journeys

## Persona 1: Priya -- Corporate HR Manager

**Role:** HR Manager at a 500-person IT company in Bangalore

**Goals:**
- Order Diwali gifts for all employees with minimal coordination overhead
- Ensure consistent quality across 500 hampers
- Track delivery status for multiple office locations
- Stay within the approved budget of INR 1,500 per hamper

**Pain Points:**
- Currently coordinates via email with 3-4 vendors, comparing quotes in spreadsheets
- No visibility into order status until she calls the vendor
- Needs to get approval from finance before confirming, which adds days of delay

**Tech Comfort:** High. Uses WhatsApp Business daily for vendor coordination.

### Priya's Journey

1. **Discover** -- Receives Gift Buddy link from a colleague or finds via search for "corporate Diwali gifts bulk order."
2. **Browse** -- Filters the catalog by occasion (Diwali), price range (INR 1,000-2,000), and checks available customization.
3. **Shortlist** -- Adds 3 hamper options to cart with quantities to compare indicative totals.
4. **Inquire** -- Submits an inquiry with company details, 500 units, 3 delivery addresses, and a note requesting branded packaging.
5. **Receive Quote** -- Gets a WhatsApp message with a detailed quote PDF within 24 hours.
6. **Negotiate/Confirm** -- Replies on WhatsApp with budget adjustment; receives revised quote; forwards to finance for approval.
7. **Track** -- Receives WhatsApp updates: confirmed, packing started, dispatched (with tracking), delivered.
8. **Reorder** -- Next quarter, returns to Gift Buddy for an appreciation hamper order, with her company details already on file.

## Persona 2: Ananya -- Executive Assistant

**Role:** EA to the CEO at a financial services firm in Mumbai

**Goals:**
- Source premium client gifts for 20 key accounts during the holiday season
- Each gift should feel personal and high-end
- Needs to move fast -- the CEO decided on gifting last minute

**Pain Points:**
- Limited time to research vendors
- Needs to handle varying delivery addresses across India
- Must ensure gifts arrive before specific dates

**Tech Comfort:** Moderate. Prefers WhatsApp over email for quick responses.

### Ananya's Journey

1. **Discover** -- Searches for "premium corporate gift hampers India" and lands on Gift Buddy.
2. **Browse** -- Looks at the "Executive" and "Premium Gourmet" collections. Checks delivery regions.
3. **Customize** -- Selects 2 different hamper types, adds personalization notes for branded cards.
4. **Inquire** -- Submits inquiry with 20 units across 15 different delivery addresses, urgent delivery flag.
5. **WhatsApp Chat** -- Taps the "Continue on WhatsApp" link. Sends the list of recipient addresses directly.
6. **Confirm** -- Receives quote, confirms, and provides delivery schedule.
7. **Monitor** -- Checks the order tracking page using her reference number. Gets WhatsApp alerts as each shipment dispatches.

## Persona 3: Rajesh -- Small Business Owner

**Role:** Founder of a 30-person digital marketing agency in Pune

**Goals:**
- Send appreciation hampers to his team at year-end
- Keep costs reasonable (INR 800-1,200 per hamper)
- Add a personal touch with a custom note

**Pain Points:**
- Doesn't have an admin team to handle logistics
- Previous experience with gifting vendors was slow and impersonal
- Needs a simple process he can handle between meetings

**Tech Comfort:** High. Runs his business largely through WhatsApp.

### Rajesh's Journey

1. **Discover** -- Sees a Gift Buddy post shared in a business WhatsApp group.
2. **Browse** -- Quickly scans the catalog on mobile. Filters by price (under INR 1,500).
3. **Select** -- Picks the "Sustainable Onboarding Hamper" for 30 units. Adds a custom note.
4. **Inquire** -- Fills the inquiry form on mobile in under 2 minutes. Single delivery address (his office).
5. **WhatsApp Confirmation** -- Gets an instant inquiry confirmation on WhatsApp. Receives a quote within hours.
6. **Quick Confirm** -- Replies "Confirmed" on WhatsApp. Done.
7. **Delivery** -- Receives dispatch and delivery notifications. Hampers arrive at the office.

## Journey Summary

| Stage | Priya (HR) | Ananya (EA) | Rajesh (SMB) |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Discovery | Referral / Search | Search | Social / WhatsApp |
| Browsing | Filter-heavy, compare | Collection-based | Quick mobile scan |
| Inquiry | Detailed, multi-address | Detailed, urgent | Simple, single address |
| Communication | WhatsApp + email | WhatsApp primary | WhatsApp only |
| Order Size | 100-500 units | 10-50 units | 10-30 units |
| Key Need | Budget compliance | Speed and premium feel | Simplicity |
