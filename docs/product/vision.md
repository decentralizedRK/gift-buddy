# Product Vision -- Gift Buddy

## Vision Statement

Gift Buddy is a premium corporate gifting platform that enables businesses to discover, customize, and order curated gift hampers through an intuitive web storefront, with WhatsApp-based order management as the primary communication channel.

## Problem

Corporate gifting in India is fragmented. HR managers, executive assistants, and business owners spend significant time coordinating bulk gift orders across email chains, phone calls, and spreadsheets. There is no single platform that combines a polished product catalog with a conversational order workflow that fits how businesses actually communicate -- via WhatsApp.

## Solution

Gift Buddy provides:

1. **Curated Catalog** -- A hand-picked selection of gift hampers designed for corporate occasions: onboarding, appreciation, festivals, wellness, and milestones.
2. **Streamlined Inquiry Flow** -- Visitors browse, build a cart, and submit a corporate inquiry with quantity, delivery details, and personalization notes -- no account required.
3. **WhatsApp-First Order Management** -- After inquiry submission, order coordination (quotes, confirmations, status updates, delivery notifications) happens through WhatsApp using approved message templates.
4. **Owner Admin Panel** -- A single-owner management interface for catalog, orders, customers, and communications.

## Target Market

- Mid-size and large companies in India ordering 10-500+ gift hampers for employees, clients, or partners.
- Small businesses ordering 5-50 hampers for team events or client appreciation.
- Executive assistants and office managers handling gifting logistics.

## Success Metrics

- Inquiry-to-order conversion rate > 40%
- Average order value > INR 25,000
- Repeat customer rate > 30% within 12 months
- WhatsApp message delivery rate > 95%
- Customer satisfaction (post-delivery survey) > 4.5/5

## Differentiators

- WhatsApp-native order communication (not just email)
- Designed specifically for corporate bulk orders, not individual retail
- Transparent indicative pricing with personalized final quotes
- Curated, not marketplace -- quality-controlled hamper selection

## Constraints

- V1 operates as a single-vendor platform (one owner/admin)
- No online payment processing in V1; orders confirmed via WhatsApp/offline
- Firebase Spark (free) plan where possible; Blaze required for Cloud Functions and outbound API calls
- WhatsApp integration requires Meta Business verification and approved templates
